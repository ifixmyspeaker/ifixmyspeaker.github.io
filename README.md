🔊 Fix My Speaker

Fix My Speaker is a free, browser-based acoustic utility designed to help remove trapped water, dust, lint, and moisture from smartphone and other device speakers using carefully generated sound frequencies.

🌐 Website: https://ifixmyspeaker.com/

No app installation is required. The tool runs directly in a modern web browser and supports phones, tablets, smartwatches, earbuds, laptops, and other devices with built-in speakers.

💦 Remove Water From Your Speaker

If your phone has been exposed to rain, splashes, spills, or accidental immersion, the 165 Hz Water Eject mode can be used to help dislodge moisture from the speaker chamber.

Recommended procedure:

Dry the outside of the device with a soft, lint-free cloth.

Disconnect chargers and other cables.

Set the media volume appropriately/high.

Position the speaker openings facing downward over a towel.

Run the water-ejection cycle.

Repeat the cycle if necessary.

Use the audio diagnostic test to check speaker clarity.

🧪 How It Works

The tool uses the device's own speaker to generate controlled audio frequencies. Low-frequency sound causes the speaker diaphragm to move, creating acoustic pressure and vibration inside the speaker chamber.

The 165 Hz water-ejection mode is intended to help move liquid droplets toward the speaker opening, while the broader frequency sweep is designed to agitate and loosen smaller particles.

The process requires no physical disassembly or specialized hardware.

🛡️ Safety & Important Notes

Fix My Speaker is intended for speaker-level water and debris removal, not internal electronics repair.

Avoid:

❌ Putting a wet phone in uncooked rice

❌ Using a hairdryer or heat gun directly on the device

❌ Blasting speaker openings with high-pressure compressed air

❌ Pushing needles, pins, or other sharp objects into speaker grilles

❌ Charging a device while its charging port is still wet

❌ Aggressively shaking or striking the device

If the device remains completely silent, produces severe distortion, becomes unusually hot, shows signs of corrosion, or has suffered significant liquid immersion, professional device inspection may be necessary.
