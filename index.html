How Does the Fix My Speaker Tool Work?

No need to take your phone apart. Here is how sound wave physics clears water and dirt from your speaker safely.

🔊

1. The 165Hz Air Pumper

Smartphone speakers have a tiny internal diaphragm. When you play our 165Hz tone, this membrane moves back and forth with maximum force, creating rapid pulses of air that push water out of the speaker holes like a miniature blower.

💧

2. Breaking Liquid Surface Tension

Water droplets love to stick to the fine mesh grille because of liquid surface tension, causing muffled or quiet audio. The rapid acoustic vibration shatters that bond, allowing droplets to bead up and roll out freely.

🌪️

3. Frequency Sweeps for Dust

Pocket lint and dust come in different sizes and stickiness. Our Dust Removal Mode sweeps from 200Hz to 1500Hz, shaking loose both heavy debris and light micro-particles from inside the acoustic cavity.

How to Eject Water from Phone Speakers (5 Easy Steps)

Follow this simple checklist right after your phone gets wet to prevent long-term hardware damage.

Step 1

Dry the Outside

Wipe your phone thoroughly with a dry cloth or paper towel. Do not plug in a charger or lightning/USB-C cable while moisture remains.

Step 2

Crank Volume to 100%

Make sure your phone is not on silent or vibrate mode. Turn media volume all the way to maximum to give the sound wave enough pushing power.

Step 3

Face the Speaker Down

Hold your phone or lean it upright so the speaker grille faces down toward a paper towel. Let gravity assist the water droplets as they exit.

Step 4

Press Start Cleaning

Play the Water Eject mode for 60 seconds. You may see tiny droplets misting or rolling onto the towel. Repeat 2 to 3 times if needed.

Step 5

Test Speaker Clarity

Use our built-in audio sound test below to check voice, treble, and stereo clarity. If sound is still slightly muffled, run the dust sweep once.

Emergency Water First-Aid: Do's and Don'ts

What to do immediately if your phone was dropped in the sink, pool, or toilet.

✅ Immediate Actions (Do This)

  • Turn off the phone immediately if it was submerged, to prevent electrical short circuits.
  • Dry the exterior with a clean microfiber towel or lint-free paper cloth.
  • Remove phone case & SIM tray to let any trapped interior humidity escape.
  • Point speakers downward and gently tap the phone against your hand to shake out loose droplets.
  • Run Fix My Speaker 2 to 3 times to blast out residual water trapped in the speaker mesh.

❌ Critical Mistakes (Never Do This)

  • NEVER put your phone in raw rice: Both Apple and Samsung warn against this. Rice dust and starch seep into ports and create a sticky, corrosive paste when mixed with water.
  • NEVER plug in the charger until the phone has dried for at least 5 to 6 hours. Charging wet ports causes instant permanent short-circuits.
  • Do NOT use a hairdryer or oven: Heat destroys the water-resistant rubber adhesive and pushes liquid deeper onto the motherboard.
  • Do not shake the phone violently: Aggressive swinging spreads water droplets into unsealed camera and microphone areas.
