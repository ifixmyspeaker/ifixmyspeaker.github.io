Emergency Liquid Exposure: Do's and Don'ts

Follow these manufacturer-backed procedures if your phone was just dropped in liquid.

✅ Immediate Actions (Do This)

  • Turn off the phone immediately to prevent electrical shorts.
  • Dry the exterior with a lint-free towel or microfiber cloth.
  • Remove the protective case and eject the SIM tray to release trapped humidity.
  • Angle the speaker grill downwards and gently tap the phone against your palm.
  • Run this 165Hz tool 2 to 3 times to expel residual liquid droplets.

❌ Fatal Mistakes (Never Do This)

  • Never charge the phone until completely dry (minimum 5 hours).
  • Do NOT put the phone in raw rice: Rice starch, flour, and silica dust enter ports and form a concrete-like corrosive paste.
  • Never use hairdryers or heat guns: Intense heat melts water-resistant rubber gaskets and pushes liquid deeper into the motherboard.
  • Do not shake violently: Violent swinging disperses water to unsealed camera and microphone cavities.

The Acoustic Engineering Behind the Tool

How sound wave physics removes moisture without physical disassembly.

📐

1. Resonance Frequency

Micro-speakers in smartphones possess natural mechanical resonance between 160 Hz and 175 Hz. Driving the speaker at exactly 165 Hz induces the largest possible cone displacement (excursion) with minimal electrical wattage.

💨

2. Acoustic Air Displacement

As the diaphragm vibrates back and forth rapidly, it creates localized positive and negative pressure waves inside the acoustic chamber, functioning like a micro-pump that overcomes the liquid surface tension adhering to the mesh.

🔬

3. Frequency Stepping

Fine dust particles have varying mass and adhesion coefficients. The dust mode steps from 200Hz to 1500Hz, shaking loose both heavier debris (low frequencies) and light particulate matter (high frequencies).
