About Fix My Speaker

Fix My Speaker (ifixmyspeaker.com) is an open-access, browser-native acoustic maintenance and diagnostic utility based on sound wave physics. Established to combat destructive internet myths—most notably the dangerous raw rice immersion myth—and to replace predatory subscription-based mobile apps, Fix My Speaker provides anyone with a web browser an instantaneous, laboratory-grade sound ejection tool at zero cost.

By leveraging the W3C Web Audio API standard, the platform synthesizes uncompressed, low-frequency 165Hz pulse waves directly on the user's audio digital signal processor (DSP). This generates controlled acoustic radiation pressure that overcomes capillary surface tension, safely dislodging trapped moisture and particulate debris from miniature acoustic meshes without risking hardware thermal damage.

Acoustic Standards & Research Credibility

All sound generation protocols, decibel output ceilings, and frequency sweeps deployed across Fix My Speaker comply with international audio safety benchmarks:

  • IEC 60268-5 Compliance: Standard for sound system equipment electroacoustic transducers. Output gain is normalized so that voice coil thermal dissipation stays within safe limits (<85°C), preventing coil burnout on mobile phones.
  • ISO 226 Equal-Loudness Contours: Tones are tuned to human auditory sensitivity curves to provide balanced acoustic power across bass and treble diagnostic bands.
  • Peer-Reviewed Editorial Integrity: All hardware repair and diagnostic tutorials are overseen by Clara Thompson, Lead Acoustic Research Specialist, cross-referencing official Apple Support, Samsung Care, and iFixit teardown documentation.
