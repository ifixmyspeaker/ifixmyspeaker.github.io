Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

🔒 Absolute Audio Privacy Guarantee

Fix My Speaker operates 100% on client-side Web Audio API synthesis. We do not request microphone permissions, record audio, store speech, or listen to your device. All sound generation happens entirely in your local browser sandbox.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not require account registration, usernames, passwords, phone numbers, or credit card details. You can use all cleaning and diagnostic functions anonymously.

2. Cookies & Web Storage

We use standard local storage (localStorage) strictly to remember your user interface preferences (such as cookie consent acknowledgment) so you are not prompted repeatedly.

3. Third-Party Advertising & Analytics

To keep this tool free for everyone, we may display third-party advertisements (such as Google AdSense) or anonymous aggregated analytics (Google Analytics). These providers may use cookies or web beacons to serve ads based on non-personally identifiable visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

4. GDPR & CCPA Rights

Under regulations such as GDPR (EU) and CCPA (California), you have the right to request information on data collection. Because we collect zero personally identifiable information (PII), we do not sell or share personal data with data brokers.

5. Contact for Privacy Inquiries

If you have any questions regarding our privacy practices, please reach out via our Contact Us page.
