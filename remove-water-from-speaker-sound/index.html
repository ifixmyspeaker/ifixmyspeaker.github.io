The 5-Minute Emergency Protocol: What to Do Immediately

Dangerous Myths to Avoid: Do Not Destroy Your Device

Common internet myths often cause irreversible corrosion and thermal failure. Avoid these hazardous techniques at all costs.

Safe & Effective Methods

  • Use 165Hz Acoustic Waves: Uses air displacement to push water out without opening the phone or voiding warranties.
  • Room-Temperature Airflow: Place your device near a cool desktop fan to evaporate expelled droplets safely.
  • Silica Gel Packets: Seal the phone in an airtight container with desiccants to absorb residual humidity.
  • Downward Gravity Positioning: Always angle speaker openings down so water drains out instead of soaking inward.

Harmful Myths to Avoid

  • Do NOT Put Your Phone in Rice: Uncooked rice contains fine starch powder that mixes with water into a corrosive glue, clogging ports permanently.
  • Do NOT Use Hair Dryers or Heat Guns: Excessive heat melts waterproof adhesive seals, warps speaker diaphragms, and damages lithium batteries.
  • Do NOT Insert Q-tips, Pins, or Needles: Sharp objects puncture the delicate acoustic mesh membrane, ruining water resistance forever.
  • Do NOT Blast with Compressed Air: High-pressure air forces moisture deeper into internal microphones and camera modules.
